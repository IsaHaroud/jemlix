# Jemlix Go-To-Market & Monetization Strategy

Status: Updated 2026-10-08. Field-tested playbook for the Moroccan wholesale B2B market (Derb Omar, Garage Allal, Casablanca, Inezgane).

---

## 1. The Core Moat: Why All Moroccan Wholesale Websites Failed

Over the last decade, every startup and platform that attempted to build a B2B wholesale marketplace in Morocco failed for one specific reason:
**They expected wholesalers (*grossistes*) to register, create accounts, take studio photos, write SKU descriptions, and upload 200 products to a brand-new website with zero buyers.**

Wholesalers laughed and went back to Telegram and WhatsApp because:
1. They have neither the time, staff, nor technical interest.
2. They do not invest labor into unproven digital platforms.
3. Chat scroll—despite its chaos—has zero friction.

### The Jemlix Breakthrough
**Jemlix unlocks the supply side by eliminating 100% of supplier friction.**
The supplier never creates a web account, never writes descriptions on a computer, and never uploads products twice. They keep posting photos to Telegram, and Jemlix turns it into a structured, searchable catalog.

## 2. The GTM Offer: "99 Days Free" + Upfront Build Fee

### The Psychology of "99 Days Free" (99 أيام مجانية)
- "6 months" is too long: suppliers forget the platform is paid software, creating friction when billing starts.
- "14 days" or "1 month" is too short: wholesale restocking cycles take several weeks.
- **99 Days (~3 months + 1 week)** is memorable, distinct, and gives enough runway for retailers to bookmark the store link, place orders, and prove clear ROI.

### Why Charge for the "Heavy Work" Upfront?
1. **Skin in the Game:** Filters out non-serious resellers and scammers. If a wholesaler pays 200–400 DH cash, they value the link and will share it on their WhatsApp status.
2. **Kills Subscription Fear:** Moroccan merchants resist monthly recurring software commitments (*"علاش نخلص كل شهر؟"*). Charging only for the setup work today and making the platform free for 99 days completely neutralizes this objection.
3. **Immediate Cash Flow:** You get compensated immediately for data curation rather than waiting months to break even.

## 3. "Heavy Work" Pricing by Channel Size

In Telegram wholesale channels, **media count $\ne$ product count**. One product typically has 3 to 8 photos (different colors, sizes, packaging). 

Never quote unlimited historical imports for huge archives (e.g. 10,000 photos). Items from 1–2 years ago are out of stock (*salaw*). Always frame the import as **active, in-stock inventory** (the last 3–6 months):

| Tier | Channel Media Count | Estimated Final Products | Upfront Setup Price | Estimated Curation Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter** | **< 1,500 photos** | ~60 – 120 products | **199 DH** | ~1.5 to 2 hours |
| **Standard** | **1,500 – 3,500 photos** | ~150 – 250 products | **299 DH** | ~3 to 4 hours |
| **Large** | **3,500 – 7,000 photos** | ~300 – 450 products | **399 DH** | ~5 to 6 hours |
| **Huge Archive** | **> 7,000 photos** | Capped at last 6 months | **499 DH** | ~6 to 7 hours |

*(All tiers include the full channel export, 480px thumbnail generation, phone extraction, and **99 Days Free Store Hosting**).*

## 4. The 3-Step "Teaser Close" (Collecting Payment Before Starting)

Never ask for payment cold. Always use the **3-Product Sample Teaser**:

1. **Step 1:** Download 3–5 product photos from their public Telegram channel.
2. **Step 2:** Spend 2 minutes creating a private demo store in Jemlix: `jemlix.com/c/test-[name]`.
3. **Step 3:** Send the private link to the owner via WhatsApp or Telegram with the Darija closing script:

> « سلام خويا [الاسم]، شفت القناة ديالك وصاوبت ليك هاد النموذج الصغير باش تشوف كيداير المحل ديالك فالويب:  
> 👉 `jemlix.com/c/test-[name]`  
> إلا عجبك وبغيتي نجبدو السلعة كاملة لي عندك فالقناة ونقادوها ليك مع 99 يوم مجانية:  
> كاين فخي ديال التوجاد والترتيب فيه **299 درهم** فقط لمرة وحدة.  
> كتدوزها فـ CIH / التجاري / كاش، وفـ 24 ساعة كيكون الكاطالوغ ديالك كامل واجد وخدام. واش نوجدو ليك الحساب؟ »

**Why this converts:**
- They see their *own* products already live on a smartphone.
- 299 DH is trivial for a wholesaler (the margin on 1–2 cartons of goods).
- They gladly pay via CIH / Cash before you run the full Telegram Desktop export.

## 5. Cohort Rule: The 5–7 Supplier Batch

Do not pitch or accept 30–40 suppliers simultaneously.
- 30 suppliers $\times$ ~150 products = **4,500 products** ($\approx$ 150 hours of data entry).
- Onboarding too many at once creates delivery delays and burnout.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operate in strict cohorts:** Accept only **5 to 7 suppliers per week**. 
> Deliver their stores within 24–48 hours, ensure they are actively DMing new products to `@JemlixBot`, verify their first retailer visits, and only then open the next cohort.

## 6. Subscription Pricing: 69 DH/Month

After the 99 free days expire:
- **Monthly Fee:** **69 DH/month** (~2.3 DH/day).
- **Semi-Annual Cash Option:** **349 DH for 6 months** (~58 DH/month). Wholesalers prefer paying once in cash over remembering monthly bank transfers.

### Why 69 DH?
At 40 suppliers $\times$ 69 DH = **~2,760 DH/month**. This is not the primary profit center; it is your **operational filter**. It covers server costs, weeds out inactive channels, and legally locks in the supplier's commitment.

The real wealth is in **controlling the supply of Moroccan wholesale inventory**.

## 7. The Real Long-Term Monetization Models

Once Jemlix hosts 40 to 100+ active wholesale catalogs updated daily, it becomes the default sourcing search engine for Moroccan retail buyers. This opens 5 high-margin revenue streams:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   JEMLIX PLATFORM REVENUE EXPANSION                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  [Phase 1] ──► Upfront Setup Fees (199–399 DH) + 99 Days Free
       │
  [Phase 2] ──► Supplier Retention Subscriptions (69 DH/month or 349 DH/6mo)
       │
  [Phase 3] ──► High-Margin Monetization:
                 ├── 1. Sponsored Placement ("Top of Category" / Hero ads)
                 ├── 2. "Jemlix Daman" (Intercity Escrow & Inspection: 3–5%)
                 ├── 3. Bulk Logistics & Carrier Kickbacks (Ghazala/CTM)
                 ├── 4. VIP Retailer Sourcing Subscriptions
                 └── 5. B2B Merchant Supplies (Custom packaging, cartons)
```

### 1. Sponsored Placement ("Top of Category")
- Wholesalers pay **300 to 500 DH/month** to pin their store at the top of their category (e.g. Shoes, Abayas, Cosmetics) or on the homepage banner.
- 10 sponsored stores = **3,000 – 5,000 DH/month** at near 100% margin.

### 2. "Jemlix Daman" (Intercity Escrow & Deal Inspection)
- **The problem:** Retailers in distant cities (Agadir, Oujda, Tangier, Laayoune) fear sending 10,000–30,000 DH cash to a Casablanca grossiste without seeing the goods.
- **The solution:**
  1. The buyer pays Jemlix.
  2. Jemlix verifies the packages in Casablanca and drops them at Ghazala / CTM.
  3. The supplier receives their payout upon carrier handover.
  4. Jemlix takes a **3% to 5% commission** on bulk orders (**500 to 1,500 DH per deal**).

### 3. Logistics & Carrier Partnerships
- Wholesale produces massive freight volume (*colis / cartons*).
- Negotiate volume rates with carriers (Ghazala, CTM Messagerie, SDTM, Kargo):
  - Offer retailers discounted bulk shipping rates (e.g. 35 DH instead of 50 DH/carton).
  - Collect a **3 to 5 DH kickback per carton** directly from the carrier.

### 4. VIP Buyer / Drop-Shipper Access
- E-commerce sellers, TikTok media buyers, and boutique owners need fast, verified stock.
- Offer a **VIP Buyer Pass** (e.g. 99 DH/month):
  - Instant alerts when new trending items are published to Jemlix.
  - Direct factory price comparisons.
  - Sourcing requests: buyers request an item, Jemlix routes it to matching grossistes.

### 5. Merchant B2B Upsells
- Wholesalers and retailers consume endless supplies of packaging:
  - Branded adhesive tape (*scotch personnalisé*), custom boxes, shipping flyers.
  - Thermal label printers and barcode rolls.
- Partner with local manufacturers to fulfill these recurring supplies directly through Jemlix.
