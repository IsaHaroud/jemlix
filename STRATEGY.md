# Jemla go-to-market playbook

Status: strategy note, 2026-10-03. Patience over randomness: win one niche first,
then expand across Morocco.

## The pitch

One sentence: **"Keep posting in your channel exactly like today — I'll turn it
into a clean store link you can share."**

- Zero behavior change, zero risk for the supplier.
- Their channel stays theirs, their customers stay theirs.
- If they quit, nothing is lost — that is the objection-killer.

## Start deep, not broad

"All jemmla across Morocco" is the vision, not the first step.

1. **One niche, one city, 5 suppliers.** Whatever is known best (e.g. textiles
   in Casa, or cosmetics). 5 deep stores beat 50 empty ones.
2. **Do everything for them.** Export their channel, build the catalog, send the
   link: "this is your store, made from your channel, free." They see the
   benefit before paying a dirham — a searchable link instead of endless chat
   scroll, something professional for their WhatsApp status and retailers.
3. **Get one measurable win.** One retailer ordering through a store link.
   Screenshot it. That screenshot convinces the next 20 suppliers more than any
   speech.
4. **Then charge.** After the trial proves itself: monthly sub, and/or
   per-product for the catalog build. People pay to keep something that already
   works, not to try something unproven.

## Handling the real objection

"I already sell fine on Telegram/WhatsApp." Answer: true — and old products die
in chat scroll. A retailer looking for last month's item can't find it. The
catalog is their archive that sells while they sleep, built from posts they
already made. No new work, recovered sales.

## Patience, operationally

- Visit in person with the demo on the phone. Show a real store, not slides.
- First suppliers should be people who already trust you — even 2-3. Momentum
  is everything.
- Keep the trial generous (14 days + first 100 products free) and the exit
  free: pause anytime, channel untouched.

## App support (already built)

- Trial status, due-date tracking, needs-attention list in Overview.
- Store-live notice with full `jemlix.com/c/<slug>` link sent automatically.
- Pause hides store + products publicly, instantly.
- Record trial / onboarding fee / monthly payments per supplier.

## Open decisions

- Launch offer numbers (free quota + monthly price) — to be encoded as an
  enforced quota (products published vs. free limit + Overview warning),
  currently handshake-only.
- Optional later: `auto_publish` flag for trusted suppliers (manual review
  stays the default).
